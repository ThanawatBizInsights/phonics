# 03 · Level 1 Lesson Plan and Page Script (s, a, t, p)

Level 1 has **5 pages**. They follow the plan's Mon–Fri rhythm, but any page can be opened on any day. Every page uses the same five stages, so Iris always knows what comes next.

| Stage | Stone label | Purpose | Budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **ears** | Review sounds (or warm up the ears on Page 1) | 1–2 min |
| 2 | **sounds** | Watch and hear a sound (Sound Card, mouth cue, action) | 2–3 min |
| 3 | **try** | The page's activity, on screen or off screen | 4–5 min |
| 4 | **words** | Blend and segment | 3 min |
| 5 | **sticker** | Finish and celebrate | 1 min |
| | | **Total, including off-screen work** | **≤ 15 min** |

The child never sees these times. The Parent Area shows them as a guide.

**Audio IDs** in brackets match `05-audio-manifest.csv`. `ph_` = isolated phoneme, `seg_` = segmented prompt, `wd_` = whole word, `nar_` = narration, `fb_` = feedback.

## Shared patterns

**Hear-and-find (review game, app-checked).** Four letter cards s a t p. App plays a phoneme ("Tap the sound you hear." [nar_g_tap_sound_you_hear] → [ph_t]). Tap the right card: card lifts, [fb_thats_it]. Tap another card: it plays its own sound, then [fb_listen_again] and the target replays. 4–6 rounds; each sound comes up at least once. No score shown.

**Read-then-reveal (reading, oral, grown-up observes).**
1. The word appears big, lowercase, in a neutral card. No picture. Each letter has a small dot under it.
2. Spoken: "Say the sounds. Now say the word." [nar_g_say_sounds] [nar_g_now_word]
3. Iris may tap any letter to hear its phoneme, as often as she likes. Whole-word audio is not available yet.
4. "hear the word" (🔊 + label) fades in after **5 s of no taps** or after she has tapped every letter once, whichever comes first. There is no visible timer.
5. Tap → [wd_…] plays, then the picture slides in. With Thai help on, a small Thai meaning sits under the picture.
6. → next.

**Sound boxes (segmenting, app-checked).** An egg-carton row with 3 cups. Spoken word plays with a picture (listening task, picture allowed). "Tap a box for each sound." [nar_g_tap_boxes] Each tap fills a cup with a rice-grain dot and plays nothing (the child says the sound). After 3 taps the cups light up in turn while [seg_…] plays. If she taps 2 or 4 times: [fb_listen_again] and the cups reset. The app logs the count only.

**Ask for help (✋?).** Always bottom-right. First tap replays the instruction or model. Second tap gives a bigger hint (slower model, highlight, ghost tile). Third tap shows the grown-up tip.

**Stop early.** ⌂ → "Stop for today?" [nar_g_stop_q] → ✋ stop / ▶ keep going.

Tracing check (app): the stroke must start near the start dot and stay within a wide tolerance band (about 40 pt). Off-path strokes just fade; there's no error sound. Three completed traces per letter mark "traced" in the Parent Area. Formation quality is the grown-up's call.

Grown-up card, rice tray (EN / TH):
- "Pour rice into a tray. Iris traces each letter with one finger and says the sound while she writes, not after."
- "เทข้าวสารลงในถาด ให้ลูกใช้นิ้วเขียนตัวอักษรบนข้าว และพูดเสียงไปพร้อมกับเขียน ไม่ใช่เขียนเสร็จแล้วค่อยพูด"
