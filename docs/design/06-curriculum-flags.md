# 06 · Curriculum Flags: Edit Before Use

Source: `source/Iris-12-Week-Home-Phonics-Plan.md` (read in full). "Week" is renamed **Level** everywhere in the app. Weekday rhythm (Mon–Fri) becomes **Page 1–5** of a level.

**Eligibility rule used for every flag below.** A word may appear as *text the child reads* only if every grapheme→phoneme correspondence (GPC) in it has been taught at or before the current level, or the whole word is a tricky word introduced at or before that level. Anything else is **listening-only**: it can be heard and pictured, never shown as reading text.

Severity: **Block** = must change before it appears in a lesson. **Change** = fix or move to listening-only. **Note** = keep, but be aware.

## B. Accent decision (affects recording and later levels)

**Chosen: Standard Southern British English**, one voice for all models. Reasons: the source sequence is UK *Letters and Sounds*, the Thai hint for short o (เอาะ) matches the British rounded /ɒ/, and the plan uses British vocabulary (torch). Consequences you need to approve:

| # | Level | Item | Problem in this accent | Action | Sev |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | 9 | **bath** | British /bɑːθ/, long vowel, not short a | Replace with **moth** or **with** | Change |
| B2 | 12 | **fast** | British /fɑːst/ | Replace with **lost** or **nest** | Change |
| B3 | 10 | tricky **her** | Non-rhotic /hɜː/ | Fine as a tricky word. Note for the voice actor | Note |
| B4 | 4 | /r/ | Only at word start in Levels 1–10 words (red, rug, run), so non-rhoticity doesn't conflict | Note | Note |

If you prefer General American, B1–B2 disappear, but the o hint (เอาะ) and "dog/pot/top" vowel models must change, and "hot" /hɑt/ is no longer a rounded short o. Decide before any recording.

### Notes on C6 and C10

Both need your call:

- **C6 / C7 / C9 (function words).** "is", "has", "as", "his" and the article "a" keep showing up in sentences. The cleanest fix is to add **a, is** as tricky words in Level 5 (with **he, she, into**). That makes "she is on a bus" legal at Level 5 and "a red bug in a hot pot" legal at Level 6. Recommended.
- **C10.** Use **vet / wet** as the only on-screen minimal pair. Put **van, vest/west, vine/wine** in the ears-only game with pictures.
