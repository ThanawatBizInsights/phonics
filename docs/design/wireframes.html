Phonics App Wireframes

Level 1 (s, a, t, p) for a 6-year-old Thai learner of English. iPad landscape first. Weeks are now levels; each level has 4–6 pages a child can open freely. Source curriculum: Iris's 12-Week Home Phonics Plan.

One tap to today's lesson

  1. ▶ today (360 × 220 pt) shows this level's letters. Goes straight into the current page, resuming if half-done."Tap to start." / "Welcome back. Tap to go on."
  2. 🔁 replay repeats the instruction. Same spot on every screen.
  3. Sticker book: earned stickers only. No shop, no currency.
  4. Grown-ups: 3-second hold, a ring fills. A quick tap does nothing.

Hear it, see the mouth, copy it

  1. Letter (300 pt) or 🔊 plays the phoneme; the letter glows for its length. Nothing plays on entry except the instruction."sssss"
  2. Mouth picture: after the first play, the tip plays once. Tap to hear it again."Snake sound. Teeth together."
  3. Action: snake hand (s), ants (a), tap the table (t), blow a candle (p).
  4. Your turn appears with a mirror icon, then 4 s of quiet. No microphone, no scoring."Your turn."
  5. next appears after the first play. Never timed.
  6. Help: 1st tap, slow model with the mouth animating; 2nd tap, grown-up tip in English/Thai.

Same screens, smaller chrome

Buttons shrink to 56 pt, tiles to 72 × 84 pt. The tray stays in one row under the slots. The Sound Card's side panel moves under the letter as a swipe row. Nothing depends on the iPad's side-by-side layout.

What changed from the reference mockups

MockupProblemChange
Page 1 "Pat sat."Capital P not taughtSingle lowercase words in Level 1. Pat stays a name in narration.
Page 2 "Tap, tap! Pip naps."i, n and plural -s are Level 2 and 10Replaced with Trace. Pip arrives in Level 2 when i is taught.
Page 3 "…look at the map." cap / mat / mapoo, th, c, m, n, d untaughtReplaced with Word Builder sat → pat.
Page 4 "Pip fans the dog by the pot."f, n, d, g, o, "by", "the" untaughtReplaced with last-sound listening (pictures allowed).
Page 5 "The duck splashes in the tub."Level 4–12 contentReplaced with the reading show: sat, tap, pat, at.
Whole-word 🔊 chips from the startGives the answer before she triesWhole word only after the attempt; picture after that.

Kept: the plush penguins, pastel pond, big rounded type and butter-yellow chips as the visual direction.

Decisions needed

  1. Accent: Standard Southern British English (recommended, matches the source sequence and the short-o hint) or General American.
  2. Add "a" and "is" as Level 5 tricky words so the plan's Level 5–6 sentences become readable.
  3. Sound hunt and tissue game stay parent-led, with no camera or microphone.
  4. Names: keep Pat in Level 1; introduce Pip at Level 2.
