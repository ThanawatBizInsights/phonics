## Wording rules

- One instruction per screen, under 8 words.
- Feedback is neutral and names the next step: "Listen again.", "Try the last sound." Never "Wrong" or "No".
- Praise the action, not the child: "You changed one sound and made a new word!"
- Never print a sound as a Thai spelling. Thai may describe the mouth (ฟันชิดกัน) but the sound itself is the English recording.
- Letter names ("ess", "tee") are not used anywhere in Level 1.
